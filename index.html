Before you write

What clients ask us first.

Do you advise, or do you build?

We build. The output of every engagement is something you keep running: a model, a data lake, a set of agents, dashboards. The advice comes with it, not instead of it.

How fast do we see something?

First numbers from your own data inside two weeks. The model and the lake stay yours when we leave.

What do the LLM agents actually do?

They do the finance work your team does by hand: pull statements, match transactions, produce the month-end figures and check them against the source. A person signs off. Nothing goes out unchecked.

Is your financial analysis a quality of earnings report?

No. It is a non-expert view of what the business really costs to run, built from the raw data. Buyers still commission their own QoE. Our work means you know the answer before they do.
