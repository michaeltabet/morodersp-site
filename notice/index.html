Important notice

The scope and limits of our work

Not a quality of earnings report

Our due diligence is a commercial, data and technology review based on the information made available to us. It is not a quality of earnings (QoE) report, an audit, a review, a compilation or any other attest or assurance engagement. Moroder SP is not a certified public accounting firm and does not provide accounting services.

A non-expert view

Any financial analysis we provide is a non-expert view, prepared in good faith from the data we were given. We believe our analysis to be generally reliable, but it has not been independently verified, it depends on the completeness and accuracy of that data, and it may contain errors or omissions.

Not advice

Nothing we provide, including research, sourcing, introductions, findings or strategy, is investment, legal, tax, accounting or valuation advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any company or security. Any decision to proceed with a transaction is yours alone. You should rely on your own independent legal, tax, accounting and financial advisers before acting.

Use of AI

Our work uses large language models and software agents. We review their output and trace figures to source documents, but automated analysis can be wrong, and our findings should be read with that in mind.

Liability

The terms of each engagement, including any limits on our liability, are set out in the engagement letter that governs it. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Moroder SP accepts no liability for any loss arising from reliance on its work by anyone other than the client named in that letter, or beyond what that letter provides.
